Motivation: 音频驱动的单人三维头部动画在真实感上已取得显著进展，但双人对话场景仍困难，因为现有对话头部生成方法忽视了两位参与者之间非语言行为语义的建模，导致缺乏交互协调性

Method: 将两路音频拼接后联合解码，使每位参与者的运动以对方为条件，把交互纳入生成过程本身；引入注视对方、摇头、点头、微笑四个离散非语言线索，通过 product-of-experts 后验在共享隐空间与连续参数融合，以线索承载交互语义并约束连续运动生成；用基于流的先验替代标准高斯先验，使隐运动具备时间依赖性；同时构建 CANDOR-FG 语料库，由 CANDOR 派生并补充双方同步 FLAME 参数与三维注视

Result: 在 CANDOR-FG 上，DiaHeads 取得有竞争力的个体保真度，并在人际协调性上较单说话人与双人基线有显著提升；消融实验证实联合生成、流式先验与多线索监督是协调性的来源；用户研究表明其优势来自可感知的交互协调而非单纯的原始运动保真度

Conclusion: 将交互语义显式嵌入生成过程是实现协调双人对话头部动画的关键，联合建模与结构化非语言线索的融合可有效提升人际协调性

Abstract: Audio-driven 3D head animation has made considerable progress in generation realism, yet generating coordinated behavior for two-person dialogue remains difficult, because current dialogue head generation methods overlook the modeling of non-verbal behavior semantics between the two participants. To address this limitation, we present DiaHeads, a framework that generates the behavior of both participants jointly. The two audio streams are concatenated and decoded together, so each participant's motion is conditioned on the other, which makes the interaction part of the generation itself. We further introduce four discrete non-verbal cues, gaze-on, head shaking, nodding, and smiling, and fuse them with the continuous parameters in a shared latent space through a product-of-experts posterior. These cues carry the interaction semantics and constrain how the continuous motion is generated. A flow-based prior over the shared latent variable replaces the standard Gaussian prior and makes the latent motion temporally dependent. These designs therefore yield coordinated 3D dialogue heads. To support this task we build CANDOR-FG, a dialogue corpus derived from CANDOR and augmented with synchronized FLAME parameters and 3D gaze for both interlocutors. Experiments on CANDOR-FG show that DiaHeads achieves competitive per-participant fidelity and substantial gains in interpersonal coordination over single-speaker and dyadic baselines. In addition, ablations confirm that joint generation, the flow-based prior, and multi-cue supervision are the sources of this coordination. Finally, a user study shows that the advantage of DiaHeads comes from perceived interactional coordination rather than from raw motion fidelity alone.
