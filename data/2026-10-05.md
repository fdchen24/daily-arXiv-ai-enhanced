### [100] [From Expression to Reaction: Role-aware Visual Transfer and Stimulus-guided Reasoning for Interlocutor Emotion Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03016)
*Wei Wang,Zhaowu Li,Jianjie Luo,Fu Lee Wang,Lap-Kei Lee,Zhenguo Yang*

Main category: cs.MM

Motivation: 听者情绪识别面临两个核心问题：一是缺乏听者标注数据导致的监督不匹配；二是听者反应在视觉上相似，其情绪含义需依赖说话人语境才能区分。

Method: RASG由两个模块构成。角色感知视觉迁移（RVT）：先筛选面部表情与情绪标签一致的说话人样本，再过滤听者轨迹，用可靠伪标签训练以听者为中心的视觉专家。刺激引导边界推理（SBR）：仅在视觉模型不确定时启用二分类语言推理器，将说话人音频与文本作为上下文而非直接情绪证据，以区分相似的听者反应。

Result: 在MER-Cross数据集上取得76.25%的性能，较基线提升超过17%；在ACM MM 2026 MER Grand Challenge Track 1（MER-Cross）中团队排名第二。

Conclusion: 角色感知迁移与上下文引导推理能有效缓解标注缺失与视觉歧义问题，验证了在听者情绪识别任务中的有效性。

Abstract: In this paper, we propose a Role-aware Stimulus-guided (RASG) framework for interlocutor emotion recognition, which predicts listener emotions from listener-only videos and speaker-only audios. RASG consists of Role-aware Visual Transfer (RVT) and Stimulus-guided Boundary Reasoning (SBR) modules, which address supervision mismatch due to the lack of labeled listener data and ambiguity among visually similar listener reactions whose interpretation depends on speaker context, respectively. More specifically, RVT selects speaker samples whose facial expressions support their emotion labels. It then filters listener tracks and uses reliable pseudo-labels to train a listener-centric visual expert. SBR uses a two-class language reasoner only when the visual model is uncertain. It treats speaker audio and text as context rather than direct emotion evidence to distinguish similar listener reactions. Experiments conducted on MER-Cross dataset shows that RASG achieves 76.25\% on MER-Cross and improves the performance of the baseline over 17\%. Our team ranks second in Track 1 (MER-Cross) of the MER Grand Challenge at ACM MM 2026.
